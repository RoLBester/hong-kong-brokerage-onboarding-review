# Proposed reminder for Futu applicants

An in-app reminder would show eligible Futu applicants a welcome-offer registration link on their first visit to the Hong Kong dollar Faster Payment System (FPS) instructions, immediately above the bank details. Applicants could register at that point before making the opening transfer.

## What the evidence shows

Futu collects the application, verifies the phone and takes an electronic signature. The applicant then links a personal Hong Kong bank account, sends at least HK$10,000 by FPS and returns to Futubull to submit the amount. This completes the application for review. Webull also uses a same-name HK$10,000 transfer to verify identity. Its FPS instructions require the amount **and a receipt** before final application submission. [Futu guide][fg] · [Webull guide][wg]

Of 20 referral-page comments, 10 concerned reward claims (50%). Asset holding, offer registration and qualifying deposits each drew three (15% each). One concerned new-user eligibility (5%). Nineteen came from Fly for Miles and one from Mr. Miles. [Coded comments](data/feedback.csv)

One reader transferred money before entering a referral code and wondered whether the app’s acceptance meant they qualified. Another saw approved funding alongside a pending application. A third asked when funding was due and whether it could be split. These earlier referral offers had their own rules. For Futu’s November offer, registration starts the deposit clock and a late registration cannot recover a missed reward. ## Proposed in-app message

> Want the November welcome reward? Register on the offer page before you transfer. Your opening payment can count towards HK$10,000 in net deposits within 15 days of registering. After you pay by FPS, return here and submit the amount.
>
> **Register for the offer**

The button opens the official offer page. At this Standard Reward tier, Futu issues a locked reward on day 16. It unlocks after average daily assets reach at least HK$10,000 over the 60 days starting the day after issue. [Reward terms][terms]

## Proposed A/B test

**Assignment.** Eligible applicants who have yet to register for the offer would be randomly assigned 50/50 on their first visit to this screen, before funding begins. A would see the existing flow. B would see the same flow with the message and link. The test would include applicants for a new Universal Account aged 18+ with a Hong Kong ID or overseas passport, no Futu Securities account or deposit before 1 November 2026 at 00:00 HKT, and no incompatible promotion. [Eligibility][terms]

**Primary metric.** The rate for each version is the share of **all assigned applicants** who open a Universal Account, register for the offer and have at least HK$10,000 in qualifying net deposits at the cutoff. Net deposits are eligible cash, stock and crypto inflows minus eligible outflows posted from offer registration through the cutoff. The opening FPS transfer counts once Futu has received the notification, approved the transfer and credited the funds. [Deposit rules][terms]

**Test window.** The cutoff is the earliest of 15 calendar days after assignment, 15 days after offer registration and the campaign close on 30 November 2026 at 23:59 HKT. A full 15-day follow-up during the November campaign would have required assignment to end by 15 November at 23:59 HKT, before the 25 November review. The proposed analysis would compare the two rates in percentage points with a 95% confidence interval and check account-opening completion for added friction.
